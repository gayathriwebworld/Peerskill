Common questions

Is PeerSkill really free?

Yes. Members pay with knowledge, not money. You teach something and learn something in return.

What if my match does not know what I want to learn?

You can still connect. PeerSkill highlights perfect matches first, but one-way matches are shown too, so you can find a person who is happy to teach in return for something else.

Do I have to be an expert?

No. Beginners and intermediate learners can teach what they know to someone starting out.

Where do sessions happen?

All sessions are online. Agree on a meeting link in chat, and share PDFs or slides in Messages or on the Materials page to prepare.
